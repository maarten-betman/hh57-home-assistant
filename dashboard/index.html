About these numbers
  • Measured by smart plugs in Home Assistant, multiplied hour by hour by the Zonneplan dynamic tariff (taxes included). Yearly figures are scaled up from the days each plug has been measuring.
  • "Cheapest hour within 24h" assumes perfect timing; a delay start that lands between 11:00 and 15:00 gets close to it.
  • Not included: the heat pump and the car. Neither has a plug meter, so their electricity use can't be measured separately.
